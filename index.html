The test

When the market hit back, we owned our mistakes

  • Our pioneer faded. Innovators grew price-sensitive and our first-mover brand fell to 1% share.
  • One forecast felt certain. Over-production cost $3.7M to hold stock, then $3.0M to clear it.
  • We spread thin across five brands just as nine rivals arrived.

We changed how we decided: predict each rival, stress-test every option, commit only when all five agree.

The comeback

Four fixes, one record year

Aim where customers go

Repositioned MERCY toward where Adopters and Innovators were heading.

37% of Adopters · $104M contribution

Rebuild the cash cow

Redesigned MOST to match the Savers' ideal on every attribute.

53% of Savers' first choice · +67% units

Focus the firepower

Every brand got one job and a matching budget.

80% of ad spend behind two stars

Right people, right stores

Sales teams sized to each brand's main channel, and no bigger.

+10 staff ≈ +0.3 pts, so we stopped

Getting brands named when customers ask AI

Buyers now ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity what to buy before they visit a website. I audit what those answers say about a brand, trace the sources behind them, and plan how to change them. Client names are withheld.

My role
Audit and strategy author (telecom); research and deck support (healthcare)
Methods
Buyer prompt testing, source and schema audits, local-language checks
When
October 2026
Engagement 1 · a mobile network in Southeast Asia

Recommended in only 3 of 24 buyer questions

The brand had newer network awards than its rivals, but AI answers were still reading its 2022–23 story.

  • A rival was the default answer in 11 of 24 prompts, thanks to its own question-shaped blog pages.
  • The evidence was stale. In 10 of 19 prompts that named the brand, every mention predated 2025.
  • Local languages were worse: absent from 8 of 15 local-language answers, against 2 in English.
The strategy I wrote

Win where the brand has a fact, concede where it doesn't

Claim five answer clusters

Each built on a fact the brand can defend, such as price per gigabyte or home internet without fibre.

Target: 3 → 9 of 24 in 90 days

Publish answers, not promos

Twelve question-led pages, a dated proof hub, and a clean-up of every outdated claim.

Fresh content in 6 → 18 prompts

Answer the weakness honestly

Concede the fights the data says it can't win, and reframe coverage with a postcode checker.

Plus native local-language pages

Grand Seiko: Japan, in every detail

How do you win new buyers in Singapore, and get them to buy at the Marina Bay Sands boutique instead of in Japan? I answered with five sources of evidence and a capped 90-day pilot.

My role
Research, strategy and deck (individual)
Brief
Win new buyers; bring buyers to the boutique
When
October 2026
52Reddit respondents across 6 communities
2 + 2expert interviews and mystery shops
60AI answers to 10 buyer questions
0 of 3AI answers without web search that placed the boutique correctly
From the deckTap to enlarge
What I found

People choose the watch, then choose where to buy it

  • Desire comes from the craft: the dial, the finishing and the gliding Spring Drive hand.
  • The sale leaks to Japan, where popular models cost 10–20% less after the tax refund.
  • The boutique doesn't show the craft yet, and some first visits feel intimidating.
What I recommended

Quiet mastery, made visible

Make it easy to discover

Short videos of the craft, content for milestone moments, and correct boutique facts in search and AI.

6 of 20 bought for a milestone

A Closer Look sessions

Bookable hands-on sessions with newcomer and collector tracks, and an advisor who follows up for 90 days.

"As long as I am not ignored"

A capped learning pilot

Six sessions, 48 seats, measured on boutique records. Scale only if it passes the gates.

Break-even: 6 sales at S$2K contribution

The business case

Low risk, because the money is released in stages

  • S$118K is committed from November to February. The last S$20K is released only if 2,000 customers are tracked by March.
  • No price promotions. The only discount is a S$5 member voucher, and it buys the customer's contact and purchase data.
  • Craftspeople, not celebrities: three creators for S$15K in total, against S$8K–25K for a single celebrity post.

The main problem
